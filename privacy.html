Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Purpose

Project Joker Backup is a private disaster-recovery utility operated by the owner of the Project Joker game server and its associated Telegram bot.

Google user data accessed

The utility uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. This permission is limited to files and folders created by the utility. It does not permit the utility to read unrelated documents or files already present in the user's Google Drive.

How data is used and stored

Server files, configuration, and the bot database are placed into a recovery archive and encrypted before upload. Encrypted archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive solely for backup and restoration. OAuth credentials and encryption keys are retained only on systems controlled by the owner.

Sharing and sale

Google user data and backup contents are not sold, shared with advertisers, or transferred to third parties.

Retention and deletion

The automated system retains approximately twelve weeks of backup archives. The owner can delete the backup folder in Google Drive and can revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to bdsgamesua@gmail.com.
