# Project Joker UI artwork

`menu_card_salon_night_v2.png` and `menu_card_salon_day_v4.png` are original AI-assisted background artworks generated for Project Joker on 2026-08-14 and refined on 2026-08-15 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool.

The night production prompt requested a wide dark-fantasy European card salon with a moonlit window, candlelit desk, emerald velvet and antique brass, a restrained original joker ornament, and a dark uncluttered centre for responsive Godot UI. A later precise edit replaced malformed lower-left cards with one consistent physical stack whose top card uses the original Project Joker card illustration from `Assets/Cards/VectorClassic/project_joker_crisp.png` as reference.

The day variant was derived from the corrected night composition by changing only time and lighting: clear late-morning sunlight outside the window, natural indirect light in the room, and every candle and lantern extinguished. A final precise edit removed all baked smoke while preserving the unlit candles and their wicks so the runtime particle emitters can remain perfectly aligned and independently adjustable. All prompts explicitly excluded logos, text, interface elements, watermarks, people and recognizable copyrighted characters.

The menu controls, typography, panels and interaction states are rendered separately by Godot and are not baked into the image.

`menu_night_city_v3.png` is a separate original AI-assisted 16:9 background generated for Project Joker on 2026-08-15 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The attached user concepts were used only as broad mood, composition and realistic settlement-density references. The final production edit replaces the early overbuilt palace skyline with an original believable low-rise alpine village: pitched-roof houses, a few streets, one modest church, bridges, mountains and warm windows. It retains the consistent fan of cards with the project's own Joker artwork at lower left, a neat blue-and-gold deck at lower right, bright lanterns and the menu-safe centre. Prompts explicitly excluded Velaris, identifiable locations or designs from *A Court of Thorns and Roses*, direct copies of real villages, logos, text, baked-in UI, people and recognizable copyrighted symbols.

All three backgrounds receive lightweight code-rendered ambient motion without moving creatures. The salon day uses three soft `CPUParticles2D` smoke emitters positioned directly on the three extinguished wicks. The salon evening adds smoothly diffused candle, lantern and moonlight; Night City adds a denser field of visible twinkling stars, restrained window and three aligned lantern animations, and a slowly breathing crescent halo. Light uses a continuous radial gradient rather than visible concentric circles. These effects are separate from the raster artwork and can be disabled or changed without regenerating the backgrounds.
