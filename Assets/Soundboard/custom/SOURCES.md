# Original custom recordings

`yapidar.mp3` and `yebiwe.mp3` were recorded by the Project Joker project owner
and friends. On 2026-08-10 the project owner confirmed their origin and requested
their inclusion in the game. The project owner is responsible for retaining the
participants' permission for commercial distribution.

The recordings contain strong and potentially offensive language. Their presence
must be disclosed accurately in storefront and age-rating questionnaires.
